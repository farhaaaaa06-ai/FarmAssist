Specimen Capture Tips

  • Natural, Even Lighting

    Photograph in diffuse natural light. Avoid harsh shadows or strong flash reflections on glossy leaf surfaces.

  • Focus on Affected Tissue

    Frame the lesion, discoloration, or pustules clearly in center view. Ensure crisp lens focus.

  • Single Leaf Framing

    Place the leaf against a plain or natural background. Avoid crowded bunches or non-foliar debris.

Early Detection Advantage

Diagnosing symptoms at early infection stages dramatically reduces crop yield loss and allows targeted bio-fungicide treatment before disease spreads across the field.
