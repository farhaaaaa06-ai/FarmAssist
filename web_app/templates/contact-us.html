Diagnostic FAQs

How accurate is the AI diagnosis?

The underlying Deep Convolutional Neural Network achieves 95% validation accuracy across standard benchmark datasets comprising 38 distinct foliar health and disease classes.

What image quality is needed?

A standard smartphone camera in good lighting is sufficient. For optimal results, ensure the leaf fills the majority of the frame with lesions in clear focus without motion blur.

Are organic treatment alternatives provided?

Yes. Each diagnostic breakdown provides cultural sanitation methods, preventive biological practices, and both organic copper/sulfur and targeted bio-fungicide remedies.

Can I use this in field conditions offline?

Once the model weights are downloaded locally, inference executes rapidly on your local CPU or GPU without requiring external cloud API calls.

Neural Engine Specs
  • Architecture: 4-Stage Deep Convolutional Network
  • Input Resolution: 224 × 224 RGB
  • Pathology Classes: 39 (38 Foliar + 1 Background)
  • Inference Framework: PyTorch • TorchVision
